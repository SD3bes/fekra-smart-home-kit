Telemetry & Sensors INPUTS
DHT11 Sensor GPIO 14

Single-wire digital bus capturing ambient temp (27.9°C) & humidity (38.1%).

MQ-2 / Gas Sensor GPIO 34 (ADC)

Analog hazard threshold detection triggering HomeKit smoke alert and sirens.

1602 I2C Display SDA: 21 | SCL: 22

I2C parallel bus showing real-time network clock, temperature, and alert flags.

Actuation Drivers OUTPUTS
MG90S / SG90 Servo GPIO 18

50Hz PWM signal turning servo from 0° (locked) to 90° (open).

Kitchen Exhaust Fan GPIO 19

Relay / MOSFET channel automated by temperature setpoint.

Multi-Room Lights (1–6) GPIO 23, 25-27, 32-33

Mapped as discrete HomeKit LightBulb accessories with voice dimming logic.

Integrated System Capabilities

Siri Voice & Apple HomeKit

Direct integration using Apple's HomeKit Accessory Protocol (HAP). Control individual room lights, kitchen lights, and trigger composite scenes such as "Good Morning" and "Good Night".

Smart Access & Actuators

Equipped with an SG90/MG90S servo-motorized entrance door that opens and closes on voice command or dashboard button, with visual position feedback.

Blynk IoT Telemetry & Autopilot

Live gauges monitoring ambient temperature (DHT11), humidity, and combustible gas levels (MQ-2). Features autonomous fan triggers when temperatures exceed predefined setpoints.
